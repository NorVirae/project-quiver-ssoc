# Computers files and power interruptions

**Your outcome:** use the shared workstation responsibly and leave files and equipment ready for the next student.

## Begin at the workstation

Inspect the desk, monitor, keyboard, cables and power connections. Report damage, overheating or loose outlets. Keep liquids, food, batteries and soldering work away from computer desks.

Adjust the chair and screen for a comfortable position and take short movement breaks. Keep ventilation openings clear. Use your assigned account and approved software; do not share passwords or disable security controls.

## Protect the work

- Save files in the approved team location. Use meaningful names containing the part or task and revision.
- Keep a dated working copy and use the agreed backup or version-control method.
- Record changes to CAD(STEP, STL, Assembly), code and parameters. Preserve the approved baseline.
- Do not delete, rename or overwrite another team's work without agreement.
- Do not connect unknown USB devices or install unapproved software. Ask before connecting equipment to the university network.
- Keep passwords, keys and personal student records out of public repositories.

## If power or network service fails

Save work if the system permits. Follow staff instructions for orderly shutdown. A UPS can remain energised after mains power fails; do not open it or repair its wiring.

Do not repeatedly switch equipment on and off. Staff inspect and release affected printers, supplies and other equipment before restart. Check restored files against the last saved revision. Report a failed backup or corrupted file.

## Before leaving

Save and back up the work, record its location, close the authorised session and lock or log out. Return shared peripherals and report faults. Do not switch off a shared circuit that supports an active authorised job.
