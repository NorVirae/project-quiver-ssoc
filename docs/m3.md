# M3 Power and wiring

**Deliverable:** identified harnesses and power connections with independent inspection and test evidence.

## Prepare

Identify **3310 Main PCB**, **3320 Battery Connector PCB** and the released harness drawings. Use **HAR-0008 ESC signal FR** for the continuity demonstration. Obtain the specified wire, terminals, crimp tool and instruments. Confirm pin numbering and connector viewing direction; the TATTU pack stays disconnected.

## Work sequence

1. Match connector and wire identities to the approved drawing. Never infer function from colour alone.
2. Label both ends as specified. Keep unused terminals and offcuts separate.
3. Assemble or inspect only the harnesses released to your team.
4. Check crimps, insulation, retention, strain relief, routing and protection.
5. On isolated equipment, perform the approved point-to-point, polarity and unintended-connection checks.
6. Record expected and actual results with units and instrument identity.
7. Obtain independent electrical review before installation or power-up.

## Stop and ask

Stop for ambiguous pinouts, damaged insulation, wrong parts, a failed test or missing acceptance criteria. Do not improvise high-current joints, board repairs or test methods. The electrical supervisor controls battery connection and first power-up.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Pin identity and polarity match the drawing.
- [ ] Crimps, insulation, retention and cable routes are accepted.
- [ ] Test results are traceable to the harness revision.
- [ ] Receiving teams have confirmed their supply and connector requirements.
- [ ] No unresolved electrical defect remains before release to integration.
- [ ] Scrap and suspect parts are separated from usable stock.

## Show your understanding

Demonstrate a check on an unpowered training harness. Explain how reversing the drawing's viewing direction could produce an incorrect connection.

**Submit:** connector schedule, inspection/test record, photographs and rework history. The electrical supervisor records acceptance; a student signature alone does not release the assembly for energisation.
