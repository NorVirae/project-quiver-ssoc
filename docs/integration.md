# Integration checks and demonstration

**Your outcome:** combine accepted subsystems through controlled checks, with one person responsible for the shared aircraft.

## Four checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Evidence required | Release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Ready to build | Induction, work plan, approved revisions and interfaces | Instructor |
| 2 Subsystem accepted | Inspection, test results, defect status and handover | Relevant technical supervisor |
| 3 Ready for powered checks | Unpowered integration, supply/polarity checks, propeller removal, tool count and written stop plan | Electrical supervisor and integration lead |
| 4 Demonstration complete | Authorised test records, SITL evidence, individual explanations and remaining restrictions | Course lead |

## Before energising

Confirm that no other team is changing the aircraft. Inspect mounts, cables, connectors, retention and clearances. Verify all required unpowered electrical checks. Confirm firmware target and approved configuration. Remove and account for all propellers and loose tools.

Identify the authorised operator, observers, exclusion area and stop method. Staff choose the appropriate supply and protection for the test. The test sheet must state expected results and stop criteria before power is applied.

## During and after a test

Run only the released test. Record conditions, observations and actual values with units. For an unexpected result, stop through the approved process, isolate, report and record. Do not repeat a failed powered test without supervisor review.

After rework, repeat the affected checks and record why they were selected. Keep the failed result in the record; do not replace it with a later pass.

## Demonstrate the learning

Each team shows its accepted subsystem, one inspection result and one interface. Each student explains their own contribution. Show the simulated flight and its limitations.

**Boundary:** this course ends with supervised bench and simulation evidence. Real flight requires a separate approved aircraft, site, crew, risk assessment and compliance with current Nigerian requirements. See S10.
