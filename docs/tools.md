# Hand tools soldering and other apparatus

**Your outcome:** choose the correct tool, use the authorised method and return it in a safe condition.

## Hand tools

Check handles, jaws, cutting edges and insulation. Use the correct size and type; do not improvise a screwdriver as a lever. Secure the work and keep fingers clear of cutting and pinch points. Collect cut wire ends and fasteners so they cannot enter the aircraft.

Use only documented torque and adhesive requirements. If a value or curing time is missing, stop and obtain the engineering owner's instruction. Do not invent a setting.

## Soldering

Separate authorisation is required. Use the approved local fume extraction, eye protection, stable iron stand and work support. Work on isolated circuits. Keep the hot tip and lead clear of cables and people; never leave a hot iron loose on the bench.

The instructor demonstrates a sound training joint and the inspection method. Allow joints to cool without disturbance. Wash hands after handling solder and workshop materials. Collect solder waste through the approved route.

## Chemicals and cleaning

Read the label and safety data sheet before using an adhesive, solvent or cleaner. Use the specified ventilation and protection. Keep containers labelled and closed. Do not decant chemicals into drink bottles or combine unknown substances.

## Specialist equipment

Drills, saws, laser cutters, CNC machines, resin printers and composite cutting or sanding require separate training, controls and authorisation. General induction does not permit their use. Dusty fabrication belongs in its assigned area, away from computers and electronics.

## Return and report

Clean tools by the approved method, inspect them and return them to the labelled location. Tag and report faults; do not return a defective tool to the ordinary rack. Never remove guards or repair mains equipment.

## Practise and show

Select the documented driver for a **2313 PCB-cover fastener** and support the isolated assembly. For crimp practice, use the terminals specified for **HAR-0008** on a training sample. Solder only a separate practice coupon; do not practise on Quiver's flight boards. Return each tool to its labelled place. Sources S1, S3 and P3.
