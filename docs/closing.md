# Returning items and closing the session

**Your outcome:** leave a traceable, safe work area and an unambiguous handover.

## Close in this order

1. **Stop work in time.** Save the final task state and identify incomplete work before the session ends.
2. **Shut down or hand over.** The authorised operator follows the equipment procedure. Do not interrupt a shared supply, required extraction or active job without agreement.
3. **Isolate the assembly.** Label the energy state and outstanding hazards. Staff control flight-battery handling.
4. **Count and return.** Account for tools, components, leads and consumables against the issue record.
5. **Inspect and separate.** Return only accepted items to usable storage. Label unfinished work; report and segregate suspect items.
6. **Clean and reset.** Collect fasteners, wire ends and approved waste. Use the correct cleaning method; do not blow hazardous dust around the room.
7. **Save and report.** Back up files, record revisions and tell the next responsible person what is complete and restricted.
8. **Obtain the close check.** The assigned checker or supervisor inspects the bench. Sign out.

## A useful handover says

Assembly and revision; owner and next operator; work completed; evidence location; current power and isolation state; open faults; items missing; next permitted task; person accepting the handover.

“Finished” or “should be okay” is not enough. If a tool is missing, report it before the aircraft is closed or released. If a job continues, name the authorised person taking responsibility.

## Example

The frame is assembled but adhesive is curing. Label the assembly with the product and procedure reference, recorded application time and the specified release time. State that loading or movement is restricted until the supervisor releases it.

## Practise and show

Reset a training bench containing spare parts, a suspect cable and unfinished work. Complete the issue/return record and explain the handover to another student.

**Pass evidence:** complete inventory, correct segregation, an accepted bench and a clear record. Sources S1, S2 and S3.
