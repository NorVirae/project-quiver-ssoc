# M2 Powertrain

**Deliverable:** identified propulsion assemblies with accepted mounts, joints and cable protection.

## Prepare

Identify the **3111 Hobbywing XRotor X6 Plus motor/ESC units**, **1412 arm tubes** and **1411 folding connectors**. Compare labels with the approved motor-position map. Keep **3112 MFP 24×8.0 CW** and **3122 CCW** propellers removed, labelled and separately accounted for.

## Work sequence

1. Inspect components and identify their aircraft positions.
2. Check folding joints, locks and retention using the specified method.
3. Fit motors and associated parts with documented fasteners and clearances.
4. Route and protect cables against pinching, abrasion and tension.
5. Check the permitted movement range on the isolated assembly.
6. Label each unit and record required motor direction for later authorised checks.

## Stop and ask

Do not connect a battery or run motors at the team bench. Do not use generic ESC calibration instructions without verifying compatibility with the issued propulsion type. Stop for damaged wiring, uncertain orientation, a loose joint or a forced fit.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Propellers absent and accounted for.
- [ ] Motor identity and position match the approved map.
- [ ] Mounts, joints and locking features inspected.
- [ ] Cables protected through the full permitted movement.
- [ ] M1 accepts fit; M3 and M4 accept power and control interfaces.
- [ ] Powered rotation checks are clearly marked pending until authorised.

## Show your understanding

Demonstrate the joint inspection and identify a possible pinch point. Explain why a secure mount does not prove correct motor configuration.

**Submit:** labelled motor map, mechanical inspection, photographs and pending-test list. The mechanical supervisor accepts the assembly; the integration lead schedules any powered checks. Close with the standard inventory and bench reset.
