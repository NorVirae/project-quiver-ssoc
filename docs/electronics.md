# Electrical benches and test instruments

**Your outcome:** prepare an approved measurement without creating a short circuit or exposing others to energy.

## Before a measurement

Identify the quantity, expected range, test points and procedure. Inspect the instrument, leads and probes. The supervisor checks the circuit and setup before power is enabled.

Use only the permitted low-energy training circuit until authorised for other work. No student mains work, UPS repair or improvised high-current testing is included in this course.

## Use the correct instrument method

| Instrument | Required practice |
| --- | --- |
| Multimeter | Confirm sockets, function and range before contact. Use resistance or continuity only on isolated circuits. Never place a current-mode meter directly across a battery or supply. |
| Bench supply | The supervisor approves voltage, polarity and current limit. Keep the output off while making or changing connections. |
| Oscilloscope | Confirm the ground-reference arrangement before connecting. A ground clip can short a circuit. Use approved points; never defeat protective earth or probe mains. |
| ESD equipment | Use the approved setup for isolated electronics. A wrist strap prevents some static damage; it is not protection from electric shock. |

## Record and return

Write the expected and observed result with units, instrument identity and circuit conditions. Stop for a reading outside the approved acceptance criteria. Do not adjust a result or bypass protection to obtain a pass.

After the authorised shutdown, remove connections safely, return leads to their racks and keep probes with the instrument. Leave controls and leads in the state specified by the lab procedure.
