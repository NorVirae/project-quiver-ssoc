# Induction checks and course completion

**Your outcome:** demonstrate the daily practices individually and know what the assessor has authorised.

## Induction is observed

For every assigned item, the instructor demonstrates the actual location or procedure, the student practises, and the assessor observes. A signature confirms recorded evidence; it does not replace the demonstration.

| Practical check | Student must show |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency orientation | Exit, assembly point, alarm and contact route |
| Opening and isolation | Supplies identified and safe training sequence explained |
| Stores | Correct issue, identification, return and review segregation |
| Battery awareness | Damage recognised, terminals protected and staff route explained |
| Workstation | Safe setup, saved file, backup and account lock |
| Assigned equipment | Required pre-use, operating and shutdown checks |
| Closing | Inventory, bench reset and accepted handover |
| Subsystem | One task completed and checked against criteria |
| Simulation | Common exercise, saved log and two limitations |

Record student, task/equipment, date, assessor, evidence, result and permitted activity. Mark unassigned equipment **Not assigned**; this gives no permission to operate it.

## Results

**Pass:** correct performance within required supervision and a clear explanation. **Practise again:** a specific gap needs instruction and reassessment. **Stop:** an unsafe condition or action requires intervention before continuing.

## Complete the knowledge check

Use the [individual knowledge check](knowledge-check.md). Explain each answer in your own words. The instructor can assess this orally where an agreed learning adjustment is needed and records the evidence.

**Proposed completion rule:** at least 8/10, all safety items 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 correct, and all applicable practical checks passed. The department adopts the final rule. Further practice and reassessment are recorded. Completed personal records belong in approved restricted university storage.
