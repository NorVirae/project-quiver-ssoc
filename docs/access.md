# Access clothing and responsibilities

**Your outcome:** enter the laboratory prepared and recognise the limits of your permission.

## Before entering

- Attend the room induction. Sign in using the laboratory's approved record and sign out when leaving.
- Know who is supervising your session. Visitors need permission and an escort.
- Wear closed footwear and clothing suited to the task. Secure long hair, loose sleeves and jewellery.
- Use the eye protection, extraction and other protection specified for your task. Ask for properly fitting equipment.
- Tell the supervisor if fatigue, illness, medication effects or an access need may affect safe work. Arrange support before starting.

## What your course pass permits

A general induction permits only the activities specifically recorded by your assessor. Separate authorisation is required for soldering, battery work, powered tests and each printer or machine. Never use another student's authorisation.

Students may inspect, organise, assemble or configure the tasks released to their team. The electrical supervisor controls battery connection and first power-up. The integration lead controls work on the shared aircraft.

## Conduct around others

Keep conversation and movement calm around active work. Do not distract someone soldering or operating equipment. Ask before touching another team's assembly or changing a shared computer configuration. Anyone may call STOP; pause and let the supervisor resolve the concern.

Keep emergency doors, switches, alarms and access routes unobstructed. Never rearrange benches, cabinets or fixed equipment without approval.

## Find these places during induction

Exit and assembly point; first-aid contact; approved isolation controls; assigned cabinet; battery storage and charging area; fault reporting point; printer stop control; waste collection points.

**Local contacts and locations must be posted before the session begins.** Use the actual university arrangements, not telephone numbers copied from overseas sources.

## Practise and show

Walk the exit route and identify your assigned work area and storage. State one activity you may perform and one that requires further authorisation. The assessor records the result. Sources S1, S3 and S5.
