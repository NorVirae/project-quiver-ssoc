# M1 Structure

**Deliverable:** a correctly assembled frame with secure, accessible mounting points.

## Prepare

Identify **1111 upper plate, 1112 middle plate, 1113 lower plate** and **1310/1320 landing-gear tubes** against the released drawings. Collect the fastener list, work support and inspection tools. M1 owns the fixed structure; coordinate arm and payload interfaces with M2 and M5.

## Work sequence

1. Inspect parts for damage and check the required dimensions.
2. Lay out fasteners in labelled trays. Record quantities issued.
3. Assemble in the specified order using the documented fasteners, torque, adhesive and curing requirements.
4. Check alignment, retention, clearance and cable routes.
5. Invite receiving teams to check their interfaces before access becomes restricted.
6. Record the assembly state and any limits on moving or loading it.

## Stop and ask

Stop for a drawing mismatch, damaged member, missing value or forced fit. Do not drill, thin, bend or cut structural parts without engineering approval. Composite work needs separate dust controls and training.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Parts and revisions match the approved work package.
- [ ] Fasteners and joints meet the documented assembly and inspection method.
- [ ] Mounts, landing gear and cable routes are checked.
- [ ] M2, M4 and M5 have reviewed the applicable fit.
- [ ] Defects, curing restrictions and pending checks are recorded.
- [ ] Tools and spare components have been accounted for and returned.

## Show your understanding

Measure one selected interface and record required versus actual values with units. Explain how an incorrectly placed mounting hole affects the receiving team.

**Submit:** assembly photographs, part/revision list, inspection results and handover. The mechanical supervisor accepts the work. Each student provides one observed task and one record they produced or checked.
