# Handling Quiver's TATTU 4.0 battery

**Your outcome:** recognise a suspect battery, protect its terminals and follow the staff-controlled handling procedure.

## Before handling

The course example is the **TATTU 4.0 HV 53.2 V, 14S, 20,000 mAh, 25C LiPo smart battery** selected for Quiver. Read the actual label and record its asset ID; do not substitute the 30 Ah pack also listed in the project BOM.

**Read the label:** 14S means fourteen cells in series; 20,000 mAh is 20 Ah. The nominal energy is 1,064 Wh. **53.2 V is nominal voltage, not a charging target; 25C is a discharge rating, not a charger setting.**

Inspect visually without squeezing, dismantling or probing the pack. Look for swelling, cuts, punctures, dents, leakage, unusual heat, damaged insulation and damaged connectors. Report a dropped pack or hard landing even if the outside looks normal.

## Move and use an accepted pack

- Use the TATTU carrying handle and the lab's approved handling method. Obtain help for its weight; never lift it by its connector.
- Protect exposed terminals against contact with tools, jewellery and other conductive objects.
- Keep the pack secure and away from heat, moisture and crushing loads.
- Identify Quiver's **2211 battery slider** and approved retention. Check fit and clearance with the pack switched off, under supervision.
- Only the authorised person connects or disconnects the flight battery under the agreed procedure.

## If a pack is suspect

Stop using it and notify the electrical supervisor. Do not charge it, reconnect it to test it, open it or attempt a repair. Keep people clear. Staff decide whether and how it can be safely isolated and moved to the designated battery quarantine location.

If it hisses, smokes, vents or rapidly heats, raise the alarm and withdraw under the emergency plan. **Do not carry an actively failing battery to a cabinet or attempt firefighting.**

## Practise and show

On an unconnected, instructor-released TATTU pack, point to the label, handle, connector and indicators. Explain that an unlit indicator does not prove electrical isolation. Use photographs for damaged-pack examples.

**Record:** pack ID, observed condition, event or impact, date, reporter and supervisor decision. A student's visual check is not an independent declaration that a pack is safe.
