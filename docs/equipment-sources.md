# Equipment manuals and demonstration kit

Use these references alongside the daily procedures. The instructor checks the manual supplied with each actual unit and records its revision before practical teaching.

## E1 TATTU 4.0 battery

Course example: **HV 53.2 V, 14S, 20,000 mAh, 25C LiPo smart battery**. The 20 Ah model in the manual is TA4TCG20K1425X. Check the physical label and connector variant.

[TATTU 4.0 14S 20/30 Ah user manual](https://www.genstattu.com/content/TATTU-4.01420Ah30AhUserManual.pdf), printed pages 9–14: handling, charging, maintenance and specifications. [Official 20 Ah product listing](https://genstattu.com/tattu-4-0-20000mah-53-2v-14s1p-25c-lipo-smart-battery-pack-with-aces-plug/).

## E2 TATTU TA7200Pro charger

Selected from the chargers listed in E1. Match the manufacturer lead to the actual battery connector; facilities staff approve the supply circuit and operating power.

[TA7200Pro user manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0836/7770/8622/files/TA7200_Pro_Manual.pdf?v=1776751964), printed pages 5–9: precautions, display, connection and pause procedure. [Manufacturer support page](https://www.genstattu.com/support-page/) provides current manuals. Demonstrate the automatic start behaviour before permitting student participation.

## E3 Bambu Lab P2S printer

[P2S user manual](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/9425242c00344b0e801b6179f9a243aa.pdf), version 1.0, May 2026: safety, first print, part removal and Bambu Studio. [P2S support wiki](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/p2s).

The training job uses Quiver **2313 battery connector PCB cover**. In the engineering repository, find:

- CAD: `src/quiver/supporting_structure/equipment_mount/steps/2313_bc_pcb_cover.step`
- Mesh: `docs/Manufacturing/Assembly-Guides/assets/models/structural/2313.stl`

Use the instructor-released revision. The BOM specifies PETG-CF; nozzle, filament, plate and print settings require approval. A successful print is not aircraft acceptance.

## Demonstration tray

Prepare labelled examples of 2111/2131 attachment spacers, 3112/3122 propellers, HAR-0008 signal harness and the 2313 cover. Demonstrate Pix32 V6 identification on the isolated avionics assembly. Use photographs for damage scenarios; do not create faults in flight hardware.

These are the selected course examples, not an assertion that every item has already been purchased by the university. Record actual asset IDs and authorisations in the equipment register.
