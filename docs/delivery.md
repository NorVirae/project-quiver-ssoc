# Course timetable and instructor preparation

Use this guide to adopt the course and prepare safe, achievable practical sessions. The rules become local operating requirements only through the responsible department and laboratory manager.

## Proposed timetable

| Session | Focus | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Room walk, daily rules, storage and isolation practice | 3 |
| 2 | Computers, battery awareness and assigned equipment induction | 3 |
| 3 | Systems and individual SITL practice | 3 |
| 4 | Team plans, drawings and interface agreements | 3 |
| 5 to 7 | Six supervised build teams | 9 |
| 8 | Unpowered integration and inspections | 3 |
| 9 | Authorised powered checks with propellers removed | 3 |
| 10 | Demonstrations, individual checks and feedback | 3 |

Thirty contact hours assume prepared kits and a tested simulator. Add time for fabrication, curing, rework or reassessment. Do not promise a flight-ready aircraft within this timetable.

## Complete before teaching

Name the course lead, laboratory manager, technical supervisors and stores lead. Set group sizes according to competent supervision. Inspect the rooms and equipment.

Post the actual emergency contacts, room identifier, exit, assembly point and first aider. Assign usable stock, work-in-progress and ordinary quarantine locations, plus separate battery arrangements. Prepare labels, issue records, safe training items and approved waste routes.

Approve drawings, pinouts, torque, adhesives, print specifications, battery procedures and software baseline. Resolve the lower-plate thickness conflict and the 20/30 Ah battery variants with the engineering owner. Prepare the TATTU 20 Ah/TA7200Pro demonstration and P2S/2313 job using the [equipment references](equipment-sources.md).

## Deliver each activity

Explain the outcome and hazards, demonstrate on the actual equipment, supervise practice, observe each student and record feedback and permission. Make reasonable learning/access adjustments while preserving the safety requirement. Revisit closing and storage habits at every session.

## Assessment notes

Answers: 1 connected energy remains; 2 review location; 3 authorised inspection/release; 4 alarm and withdraw; 5 no; 6 prevent rotating-part injury; 7 status, evidence, defects and next task; 8 no; 9 no; 10 stop and obtain the approved instruction.

Review incident patterns, missing items, feedback and timings after the cohort. Record improvements, owners and review dates. Sources S8 and S9 provide educational context; this course is not independently accredited.
