# M4 Avionics

**Deliverable:** installed flight-control, sensor and communication hardware with a verified port and configuration record.

## Prepare

Identify the **3332 Holybro Pix32 V6**, **3330 flight-controller PCB** and **3312 Raspberry Pi 5** using physical labels and the approved port map. Pix32 V6 is the flight-controller board; the Raspberry Pi is the companion computer. Verify the actual hardware before selecting firmware.

## Work sequence

1. Match controller, carrier and sensor identities to the approved hardware record.
2. Confirm orientation, mounting method, clearances and cable routes.
3. Fit and label the authorised connections while isolated.
4. Review every power source with M3, including USB and companion-computer feeds.
5. Agree the firmware target, version and parameter baseline with M6.
6. List required calibrations, communication checks and integration conditions.

## Stop and ask

Stop for an uncertain board target, incorrect orientation, damaged port or conflicting pinout. Obtain approval for each power arrangement. USB may energise part of a circuit. Do not flash firmware or change settings while another team is working on the shared aircraft.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Actual hardware and selected firmware target match.
- [ ] Orientation and mounting are documented.
- [ ] Power and signal connections have independent checks.
- [ ] M3 and M6 accept the interfaces.
- [ ] Calibrations and powered checks are completed at the approved stage or explicitly listed as pending.
- [ ] Boards and sensitive components are stored in approved protective packaging.

## Show your understanding

Point out the Pix32 V6 orientation and trace one approved sensor connection. Inspect the **2311 Main PCB mount** and specified vibration isolation. Explain why a displaced mount or changed assembly requires review before calibration.

**Submit:** port map, hardware identities, mounting photographs and configuration record. The avionics supervisor accepts the installation. Return probes with their instruments and record any loose or suspect connector before closing the session.
