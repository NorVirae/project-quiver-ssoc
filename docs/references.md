# Sources and adaptation notes

The course adapts practical habits from university laboratories and applies them to a supervised Quiver build. Local procedures and the actual equipment manuals determine authorisation and operating limits.

## Daily laboratory practice

**S1 Maryland Robotics Drone Lab** — [Rules and safety](https://www.facilities.robotics.umd.edu/dronelab/safety.html). Used for assigned storage, authorised tools and returning equipment. Its local flight thresholds, emergency contacts and instructions to move burning equipment are not adopted.

**S2 Fresno State** — [Electronics laboratory safety](https://engineering.fresnostate.edu/ece/labs/labsafety.html), operating guidelines 14 to 16. Used for component trays, lead racks, keeping probes with instruments and bench reset. Its local network rules are not UNILAG rules.

**S3 Penn State Behrend** — [Laboratory and shop procedures](https://behrend.psu.edu/school-of-engineering/resources-for/current-students/laboratory-policies-for-students). Used for documented equipment training, proper return and damaged-item reporting. Local access rules and penalties are not transferred.

**S4 Queen Mary University of London** — [Research labs and drones](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/hsd/a-z/lithium-battery-safety/research-labs-and-drones/). Used for supervised charging, inspection and protected storage. UK law references and emergency numbers are not Nigerian requirements.

**S5 NUbots** — [Lab induction checklist](https://nubook.nubots.net/683a9aba1282df3bee48ec7e946e12c8/lab-induction-checklist.pdf), battery items 1.1 to 1.6 and signatures. It records instruction and acknowledgement. Our observed student demonstrations are an added Quiver course design choice. Its software ownership agreement is not adopted.

## Equipment and education

**S6 NIOSH** — [Approaches to safe 3D printing](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/publications/numbered/2024-103.html). Emissions and controls for makerspaces and schools.

**S7 ArduPilot** — [SITL documentation](https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/sitl-simulator-software-in-the-loop.html) and [motor setup safety](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/connect-escs-and-motors.html). Verify the exact hardware and software before applying instructions.

**S8 UNILAG** — [Student centred learning guideline](https://unilag.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Student-Centred-Learning-Guideline.pdf) and [university policies](https://unilag.edu.ng/policies/).

**S9 NUC** — [Engineering CCMAS](https://www.nuc.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Engineering-CCMAS.pdf). Programme context; the department determines any credit mapping.

**S10 NCAA** — [Part 21 RPAS](https://www.ncaa.gov.ng/documents/regulations/nig-cars-2023/part-21-remotely-piloted-aircraft-system-rpas/). Check current requirements before real flight operations.

## Design and provenance

Use short procedures, nearby diagrams, worked examples and repeated practice. [CDC information chunking](https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/tool/page-9.html) and [IES instructional guidance](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) inform this approach. They do not prescribe this course's assessment threshold.
